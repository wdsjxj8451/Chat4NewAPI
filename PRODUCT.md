# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who self-host a personal or small-group chat instance, and the users they invite to chat. Users read and continue their own conversations across devices. Administrators manage access and model availability.

## Product Purpose

Chat4NewAPI provides a browser-based conversation workspace for models served through New API. The server keeps accounts, chat history, and attachments in local storage.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Keep authentication, administrator access, conversation history, model selection, and existing message actions available.
- Model capabilities determine which reasoning, search, attachment, and voice controls appear.
- Support desktop and mobile browsers, light and dark themes, and the existing Chinese and English chat UI.
- Keep service credentials on the server. Conversations use self-hosted storage.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the Chat4NewAPI name. Use the familiar conversation layout and interaction patterns of ChatGPT and Claude as references for this redesign.
