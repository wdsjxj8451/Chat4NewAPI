# Chat workspace

Mode: Operate

## Direction contract

**Thesis.** A quiet space for long conversations with a composer that stays within reach.

**Own-world.** Porcelain, graphite, and iris. A three-stroke Chat4NewAPI mark ties together the sidebar, welcome screen, and assistant turns.

**Story.** Start a draft, choose an available model, and move between current and past conversations without losing your place or draft.

**First viewport.** A restrained sidebar frames an upper-middle welcome composition. The composer and transcript share a centered reading column. On small screens the top bar and bottom composer remain reachable while the conversation scrolls independently.

**Form.** Familiar chat layout per “类似与Chatgpt和Claude的前端聊天页面”. Its signature interaction is a one-time stroke reveal in the welcome mark. New messages and drawer states use brief directional motion with reduced-motion alternatives.

**Finish.** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
